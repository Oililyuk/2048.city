## 🎯 核心资产 (Core Assets)

### Logo Section
- Brand Name: `2048.city`
- Font: `Space Grotesk` (Geometric display, bold)
- Aesthetic: Silver-to-teal linear gradient clip with soft neon glows.
- Watermark / Corner Placement: Floating left header.

### The Game Grid & Tactile Boards
- Grid Structure: Strict 4x4 mathematical layout.
- Empty Cell Slots: Must remain visible as physical glass keycap indentations (inset shadow depths) rather than hidden, providing spatial alignment.
- Mode Pills: Sleek segment selector tabs for Classic, Practice, and Daily.

## 🚫 视觉禁区 (Esthetic Exclusions)
- **NO Purple Gradient AI-Slop**: Avoid generic SaaS purple/pink overlays unless explicitly called for by a brand override.
- **NO Emoji Slop**: Replace standard emojis in cards with fine-machined SVGs or geometric icons.
- **NO CSS silhouette images**: Rely on actual rendering, real pictures, or high-definition card shadows.
